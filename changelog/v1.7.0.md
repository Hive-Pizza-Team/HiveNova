- Marketplace is now in the game menu. You can post and buy resource or ship offers, cancel your own listings, and filter the list, including on a phone. Prices have to stay in a fair range, and posting costs a small deuterium fee.
- On a phone, the top bar shows unused energy again (red when you're short), and the Resources page uses compact one-row-per-source rows instead of tall stacked cards.
- Messages show unread counts and a proper empty state, and compose works. Notes opens with the full game menu. You can collapse sections in the left sidebar, and logging out now ends your session.
- On a phone, login and register no longer trigger iOS zoom, Galaxy fleet menus work in landscape, tap targets and the Register button fit better, and the language you pick on the lobby carries into the game.
- You can send feedback or a bug report from inside the game, upgrade a password account to a Hive account from Settings, and look up season medals. Switching universes is clearer, and the Defensive Posture and Hold briefings spell out the rules and counts.

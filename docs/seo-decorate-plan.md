## Decisions made

- Audience for the lobby copy: OGame veterans and casual browser gamers, ideally familiar with crypto gaming. Primary keyword: "free browser space strategy game". OGame is named once, as "in the OGame tradition" (not "clone").
- Keep "Hive" mentions sparse in lobby copy. Season ranks are in-game ranks, not Hive ranks. PIZZA tokens are tradeable on any Hive-Engine DEX.
- Team Mithril (github.com/srbde) is the Mithril side of the Hive Pizza Team and is credited as `contributor`; Hive Pizza Team stays `publisher`. Do not put Team Mithril in `sameAs` (that would claim they are the same entity).
- Ban List is `noindex` (names real players, thin content). Contact stays indexable for now.
- Footer, not a nav bar, for internal links.

## Current state

**In place**
- Title, description, canonical, hreflang (8 languages + x-default), Open Graph and Twitter cards on every lobby page, generated centrally by `PublicSeo`.
- Homepage JSON-LD: `VideoGame` (publisher Hive Pizza Team, contributor Team Mithril) and `FAQPage`.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` (6 pages x 8 languages, `lastmod`, hreflang alternates), `sitemap_index.xml`, with unit tests.
- Footer internal links; Ban List `noindex`.
- In-game header always emits `noindex, nofollow`.

**Remaining gaps**
1. Hall of Fame rows carry a report id (`rid`) but do not link to the report. Screenshots is image-only with a generic `alt`; Hall of Fame is a bare table with inline styles and no intro text.
2. Shared battle reports cannot rank (`game.php` disallowed in robots.txt, game header is `noindex`).
3. Canonical URL ignores the universe (`uniN/` rewrite), so per-universe Hall of Fame content collapses to one URL.
4. `og:locale` is `en` (spec wants `en_US`); OG image is 4:3 (1024x768) rather than ~1.91:1. No standalone `WebSite`/`Organization`/`BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD.
5. `sitemap_index.xml` only points at `sitemap.xml` (harmless). `lastmod` is a fixed date someone must refresh via the generator script.

## Phase 3: deeper decisions

These need discussion before any code.

1. **Public battle reports.** Hall of Fame reports are likely the best long-tail content, but indexing them means exposing player names in search results and changing robots/noindex rules for a `game.php` page. Needs a privacy call and likely an opt-in or anonymized public report URL.
2. **Public tools.** A public tech tree and/or battle simulator are natural link-bait ("OGame fleet calculator"), but they currently sit behind login. Needs a decision on a read-only public version.
3. **Per-universe URLs.** Decide how canonical and sitemap should treat universes (`uniN/` rewrite, `UNI`) so per-universe content is not collapsed or duplicated.
4. **Content hub.** Whether to add static guide/FAQ pages (e.g. getting started, Hive integration explained) as a rankable content layer, and who maintains them.
5. **Measurement.** Search Console setup, a baseline of impressions/queries, and a way to tell whether changes help. The footer still ships legacy `ga.js` analytics; decide whether to replace it.
